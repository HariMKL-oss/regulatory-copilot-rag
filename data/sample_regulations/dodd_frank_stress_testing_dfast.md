# Bank Internal Policy: Dodd-Frank Act Stress Testing (DFAST) & CCAR Execution Guidelines
- **Document ID**: `POL-RISK-DFAST-004`
- **Owner**: Enterprise Capital Management & Stress Testing Committee
- **Classification**: `INTERNAL_BANK_POLICY`
- **Effective Date**: March 15, 2023

## Section 1: Supervisory Stress Scenarios
### Clause 1.1: Severely Adverse Scenario Definition
The annual supervisory stress test evaluates balance sheet resilience across three macroeconomic scenarios: Baseline, Adverse, and Severely Adverse. The Severely Adverse scenario features a severe global recession accompanied by a 40% decline in commercial real estate prices, a 5.0 percentage point surge in the unemployment rate, and severe corporate bond spread widening.

### Clause 1.2: Minimum Post-Stress Capital Hurdle Rates
Under the 9-quarter Severely Adverse projection horizon, the bank's post-stress regulatory capital ratios must never breach supervisory minimums:
- Post-stress CET1 ratio must remain at or above 4.5%.
- Post-stress Tier 1 Leverage ratio must remain at or above 4.0%.

## Section 2: Capital Distribution and Governance Controls
### Clause 2.1: Planned Capital Distributions
Planned share repurchases and common stock dividend increases must be dynamically reduced if the projected post-stress capital ratio approaches within 50 basis points of the minimum hurdle rate.

### Clause 2.2: Model Validation and Challenge
All stress testing forecasting models (Pre-Provision Net Revenue, Loan Loss Provisions, and RWA Projections) must undergo independent annual validation by the Model Risk Management (MRM) group in compliance with SR 11-7.
