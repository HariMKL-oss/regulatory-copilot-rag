# Bank Commercial Credit Policy: Underwriting Standards, DSCR, and Authority Matrix
- **Document ID**: `POL-CREDIT-UW-012`
- **Owner**: Chief Credit Officer & Senior Credit Committee
- **Classification**: `RESTRICTED_CREDIT_POLICY`
- **Effective Date**: August 1, 2023

## Section 1: Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Underwriting Thresholds
### Clause 1.1: Minimum Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
All income-producing commercial real estate loans must demonstrate a minimum historical and projected Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) of 1.25x based on net operating income (NOI) after deducting market-rate vacancy allowances and capital expenditure reserves.

### Clause 1.2: Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios
Maximum permitted Loan-to-Value (LTV) limits by collateral property type:
- Multifamily properties: Maximum 75.0% LTV
- Industrial and Warehouse properties: Maximum 70.0% LTV
- Office and Retail properties: Maximum 65.0% LTV
- Raw Land and Land Development: Maximum 50.0% LTV

## Section 2: Credit Approval Authority and Policy Exceptions
### Clause 2.1: Exception Delegation Matrix
Any loan approval containing an exception to standard DSCR or LTV parameters requires formal joint sign-off by the Senior Credit Officer and Regional Credit President. No single loan officer holds unilateral exception approval authority.

### Clause 2.2: Environmental Site Assessment (ESA)
A Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) conducted within 180 days prior to closing is mandatory for all commercial transactions exceeding $1,000,000 in aggregate commitment.
