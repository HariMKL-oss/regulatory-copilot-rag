# Basel III International Regulatory Framework for Banks: Capital and Liquidity Standards
- **Document ID**: `REG-BASEL-III-2023`
- **Jurisdiction**: Global / Federal Reserve Implementation
- **Classification**: `PUBLIC_REGULATORY`
- **Effective Date**: January 1, 2023

## Section 1: Minimum Capital Requirements and Buffers
### Clause 1.1: Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)
All commercial banking organizations must maintain a minimum Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) risk-based capital ratio of 4.5% of risk-weighted assets (RWA) at all times.

### Clause 1.2: Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB)
In addition to the minimum CET1 ratio, institutions must hold a Capital Conservation Buffer of 2.5% comprised entirely of CET1 capital. This establishes an effective minimum CET1 ratio of 7.0% before capital distribution constraints (dividends and discretionary bonuses) are triggered.

### Clause 1.3: Total Capital Ratio
The minimum Total Risk-Based Capital ratio is 8.0%, which rises to 10.5% when including the Capital Conservation Buffer.

## Section 2: Liquidity Risk Ratios
### Clause 2.1: Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)
The Liquidity Coverage Ratio requires institutions to hold an amount of High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) equal to or greater than total net cash outflows over a 30-calendar-day severe liquidity stress period:
$$\text{LCR} = \frac{\text{Total HQLA}}{\text{Total Net Cash Outflows over 30 Days}} \ge 100\%$$

### Clause 2.2: Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)
The Net Stable Funding Ratio requires institutions to maintain a stable funding profile in relation to the composition of their assets and off-balance sheet activities across a 1-year horizon:
$$\text{NSFR} = \frac{\text{Available Stable Funding (ASF)}}{\text{Required Stable Funding (RSF)}} \ge 100\%$$
