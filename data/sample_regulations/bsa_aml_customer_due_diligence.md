# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & Bank Secrecy Act (BSA): Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Standard
- **Document ID**: `POL-AML-CDD-009`
- **Owner**: Chief Compliance Officer & Financial Crimes Intelligence Unit
- **Classification**: `CONFIDENTIAL_COMPLIANCE`
- **Effective Date**: June 1, 2023

## Section 1: Customer Due Diligence and Beneficial Ownership
### Clause 1.1: Beneficial Ownership 25% Rule
Under FinCEN Customer Due Diligence regulations, financial institutions must identify and verify the identity of the beneficial owners of legal entity customers. A beneficial owner is defined as each individual who owns, directly or indirectly, 25 percent or more of the equity interests of a legal entity customer.

### Clause 1.2: Control Person Identification
In addition to 25% equity owners, one individual with significant responsibility to control, manage, or direct a legal entity customer (e.g., Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Managing Member) must be verified.

## Section 2: Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR) Timelines
### Clause 2.1: 30-Day Mandatory Filing Deadline
The bank must file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) with FinCEN no later than 30 calendar days after the date of initial detection of facts that may constitute a basis for filing a SAR.

### Clause 2.2: 60-Day Extension for Unknown Suspects
If no suspect was identified on the date of detection of the incident, the bank may delay filing a SAR for an additional 30 calendar days (totaling 60 calendar days), but cannot exceed 60 days.

### Clause 2.3: Strict SAR Confidentiality (Tipping Off Prohibition)
Under 31 U.S.C. 5318(g)(2), bank personnel are strictly prohibited from disclosing to any person involved in the transaction that a SAR has been filed or that suspicious activity is under investigation.
